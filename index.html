About Vantage Realty

Real estate search shouldn't require trusting a stranger's asking price.

Our story

Built by people who were tired of guessing.

Vantage Realty began as an internal data project mapping apartment and land prices across Bangalore and Chennai locality by locality. What started as a spreadsheet to help a few friends negotiate honestly turned into a full listings platform once we realized how badly that transparency was missing everywhere else.

Today, every listing on Vantage Realty carries the same locality-level price/sqft context that spreadsheet started with. We didn't add analytics to a listings site — we added listings to an analytics tool.

We serve buyers and investors across two cities who want one thing: to know, before they pick up the phone, whether a price is actually fair.

Our mission

Price clarity, by default.

Every buyer deserves to see the same locality data an experienced local agent would quietly know. Our mission is to make that knowledge visible on every listing, not locked behind a negotiation.

What makes us different

Three things you won't find on a typical listings portal.

  • →Locality-level price/sqft on every single listing, not a citywide average that hides the real story.
  • →Apartments and land/plots shown together, so you can weigh a ready home against a plot in the same street.
  • →A refresh cycle that catches stale or sold listings, instead of leaving dead entries live for months.
